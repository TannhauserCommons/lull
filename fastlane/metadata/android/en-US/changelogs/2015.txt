* Add to queue on an album or playlist queues all its songs, waiting for a late tracklist
* Albums opened before load at once
* After the queue ends, adding, Play and Previous do what you expect
* A song queued twice is two entries, and Undo puts back what it removed
* Next at the end of a radio queue moves one song
* Play here starts where the other device was
* Queue keys: double-click jumps, Shift+Enter plays next
* Desktop Classic: repeat one, Next during a crossfade and gapless fixes
